01. Taking care of your information

This policy describes how personal information is used in Parrillas en su Casa's sales support, WhatsApp inquiries and private team dashboard. You do not need an administrator account to request a quote.

02. Information we process

Depending on your inquiry or the service requested, we may receive:

  • The contact person's name and WhatsApp number.
  • Messages and event details: date, time, location, number of guests and requested service.
  • Information needed to prepare, review and follow up on a quote.
  • For team members: name, email, role and information needed to protect their dashboard access.

Avoid sending passwords, payment card details, identity documents or sensitive information in an inquiry. Relevant event details are sufficient to estimate the service; we do not need names or identifying information about children attending the event.

03. How we use it

This information allows us to answer inquiries, confirm event details, prepare quotes and coordinate the requested service. Dashboard reminders help the team organize its internal follow-up.

Team accounts and sessions are used to control access and permissions. An inquiry does not authorize advertising campaigns or other uses unrelated to your request. Where applicable, we will ask for your explicit consent.

You can choose what information to provide. Without a sufficiently clear date, number of guests or location, the team may be unable to prepare a complete quote.

04. Who can access it

Authorized Parrillas en su Casa team members access the information needed to handle requests. We do not sell our contacts' data.

WhatsApp and Meta handle message delivery; Railway hosts the platform and its database. These providers may process information on infrastructure outside Costa Rica under their services and terms. Information may also be disclosed when required by a legal obligation.

05. How long we keep it

We keep the information needed to handle your request, provide the service and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask about retention in your case and request deletion of data that is no longer needed.

Deactivating a team account prevents further access; sales records may retain a reference to that account to preserve the traceability of operations.

06. Security and cookies

The dashboard uses authenticated access, role-based permissions and measures to protect passwords and sessions. No system can guarantee absolute protection.

Sign-in uses a necessary cookie to identify the session and protect forms. The platform does not include advertising cookies or third-party analytics. External services, such as WhatsApp, have their own policies.

07. Access, correction and deletion

You can request access to your data, correct information, request deletion where applicable and withdraw consent for processing that relies on it. You can also ask for nonessential communications to stop.

Contact the team using the channel below. To protect your information, we may verify your identity using the minimum information necessary. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law; a legal obligation may require us to retain certain records.
