01. Support for your event

These terms describe the use of Parrillas en su Casa's digital channels to inquire about barbecue catering services, request quotes and coordinate assistance. The private dashboard is reserved for authorized team members.

02. Inquiries and quotes

To prepare a proposal, the team may request the date, number of guests, location, time and service preferences. Provide accurate information and communicate any changes that affect the quote.

Prices and conditions are reviewed for each request. A draft or an initial response does not constitute a confirmed booking. The final proposal must state the scope of the service, price and agreed conditions.

03. Confirmation and changes

Availability, the menu, payment terms and event confirmation require coordination with the team. Simply using this platform does not establish a rate or deposit percentage.

Ask the team about any changes to the date, guest count or service. Modification or cancellation conditions must be communicated and agreed upon in the relevant service agreement, without limiting rights recognized by applicable law.

04. Communication through WhatsApp

If you start an inquiry through WhatsApp, the team may respond about that request, ask for clarification and coordinate the service. You can ask for nonessential communications to stop. Promotions require the applicable authorization.

Support may combine guided questions and human review. Internal team reminders do not constitute automatic messages to the customer or a booking.

05. Accounts and responsible use

Dashboard accounts are personal and assigned by the administrator. Do not share your password or use someone else's account. Tell the administrator if you suspect unauthorized access.

Use the channels for lawful purposes. Do not attempt to access other people's information, bypass security controls or interfere with the service. The administrator may deactivate team accounts when access is no longer appropriate.

06. Personal information

The processing of contact details, messages, event information and team accounts is described in the privacy policy. Provide only the information needed to handle your request.

07. Channel availability

The website and WhatsApp may experience interruptions, maintenance or delays from external providers. If you do not receive confirmation, check with the team before assuming that a request, change or booking has been accepted.

These terms do not exclude responsibilities or rights that are mandatory under applicable law.
